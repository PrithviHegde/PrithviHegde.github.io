FilingDelta In progress

Question
When management changes the wording of a company's SEC filings, does the stock price react, and how quickly?
Method
A pipeline over 88 quarterly and annual filings from 11 large caps: extract and clean the Management's Discussion & Analysis, pair filings year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter, and time each event by when the SEC accepted it rather than the posted filing date.
Status
Data pipeline done (78 filing pairs). Paragraph-level diffing and the event study come next.

2026

moss

Question
Why do plants spread faster than an average dispersal distance predicts? (Reid's paradox: oak reclaimed Britain far too fast after the last ice age.)
Method
Simulated spread under Gaussian, exponential and lognormal dispersal kernels with the same mean distance; modelled when ferns appear with Weibull waiting times.
Result
After 40 generations the fat-tailed lognormal colony reaches 14× farther than the Gaussian one, and it is still speeding up.
